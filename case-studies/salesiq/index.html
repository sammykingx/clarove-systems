The challenge

Social commerce sellers close sales in DMs, WhatsApp chats and phone calls. The money arrives, but the business behind it stays invisible.

A payment receipt only proves that someone paid. It can’t show what earns, who buys again or when the store is busiest. Merchants were growing without being able to see their own business.

What merchants couldn’t see

  • What each product actually earns
  • Who their customers are, what they spend and how often they return
  • When the store is at its busiest
  • A clear history of every sale made

What merchants needed

  • One place to record every sale, from any channel, in seconds
  • The numbers behind the business, ready every day
  • Professional receipts customers trust, and a sales trail they can follow

The solution

Clarove Systems engineered SalesIQ as an offline-first financial platform designed around zero-friction workflows. We implemented intelligent customer directory module, dynamic product catalog management, and a robust business intelligence engine that computes daily vitals effortlessly.

Every sale gets an instant receipt, shared as a link, printed at the counter or downloaded as a PDF, and kept in a permanent, searchable sales history. Merchants find any past sale in seconds, and customers never need an account or password.
