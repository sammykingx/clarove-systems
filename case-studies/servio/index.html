The challenge

Hiring service providers from video editors to plumbers usually involves informal WhatsApp chats, voice notes, and unorganized DMs. Clients struggle to compare vague pricing, while providers face scope creep and delayed payments.

Most service failures are definition failures, not execution failures. Servio is a system that converts informal hiring into structured, trustworthy business agreements.

What the business needed

  • A structured service intake system for both online and on-ground roles
  • Automated contract generation and multi-currency payout logic (NGN & USD)
  • An intuitive dashboard built for rapid decision-making without complex onboarding

The solution

We designed Servio as a dual-role service infrastructure layer. Clients create open or role-based requests, while providers submit structured, value-first proposals rather than engaging in price wars.

To eliminate financial anxiety, we built custom payout rules backed by Paystack and Stripe integrations, ensuring funds are committed upfront and released based on agreed terms.
